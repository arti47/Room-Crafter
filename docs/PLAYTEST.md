# Room Crafter — full play-through script

One sitting, phone first (about 60–90 min). It covers every control the app has
and every rule in CLAUDE.md §5. Do the steps in order: later steps rely on the
state earlier ones leave.

**How to report:** step number · what you did · what happened · what you
expected. A screenshot beats a sentence. Anything that felt slow, unclear or
wordy counts too, even when nothing is "wrong".

**Before you start:** open the app, reload once and accept the update toast if
one appears. The build should be `rc-v21` or later. Settings → Undo and erase →
**Erase everything** gives you a clean start; it offers Undo, so nothing is lost
by accident.

**Playtest mode (hidden).** The app can walk this script for you and build the
report. Settings → About → tap the **version line seven times** → set a passcode
(4–8 digits; asked once per browser session after that). Choose what may be
recorded, then **Start the playtest**. A panel floats above the bottom bar: the
step, its check, **Pass / Issue / Skip**, a note, **📷 Capture** (the app's
screen) and **📎 Attach** (a phone screenshot). Fold it to a pill while you
play. When done: open the panel → **Report** → **Build the report (.zip)**, and
attach the zip to a message for analysis. **End session** deletes everything it
recorded from the device.

Each step has the thing to do, then **Check** — what should happen. `Rn` refers
to the rule ledger in CLAUDE.md §5.

## Part A — first run (5 min)

1. Open the app on an empty install.
   **Check:** a full-screen welcome, one panel at a time, Next / Skip. The last
   panel offers "Load the example rooms".
2. Tap **Skip**. Then Learn → **Tutorial**.
   **Check:** numbered stations. Each one links to where it is done; tapping it
   opens that screen and pulses the control it means.
3. In the tutorial, load the example rooms.
   **Check:** the demo rooms load into a crawl called Examples, labelled with a
   house-aid badge as written for this app (process rule 10).
   Loading them twice resets them rather than stacking copies.
4. Learn → **Rules**. Type "two" in the search.
   **Check:** matching entries open on their own. Opening a rule from a link
   anywhere in the app gives you a Back to where you were.

## Part B — Crawl 1, Mythic on: the full procedure (30–40 min)

### B1. The crawl
5. Play → **New crawl** → name it → **Create**.
   **Check:** the crawl screen is a map with a "First room" tile. Journal and
   Print chips are hidden until there is a room.
6. Tap **Add an explorer and a goal** → fill both → Save.
   **Check:** the banner shows them with a *house rule* badge.

### B2. Room 1 — six keywords, every walk permission
7. Tap the next-room tile. Name it, type a room type or tap **Roll a room type**,
   leave "Does this room cover several spaces?" **off**, budget **Six keywords** →
   **Start the keyword walk**.
   **Check:** a rolled room type shows "Rolled: … house aid" (R30). The room bar
   replaces the app header; the stepper is on Keywords, 0/6.
8. **Roll keyword 1.** Type a name on the card → **Make this an Area** (or Enter).
   **Check (R1, R2):** one keyword at a time, numbered. The Area appears as a
   named tile on the plan below, with its keyword under the name. An empty name
   is refused.
9. Roll keyword 2 → **Nothing yet — carry it forward**.
   **Check (R3):** keyword 3 arrives on top of 2 ("Combined", two dice). The
   plan shows a dashed tile with both words.
10. With the pair on the card, look for the carry button.
    **Check (R4):** it is gone, replaced by "Combining stops at two — see why".
    Name the pair → Make this an Area. One Area, two keywords used.
11. Roll 4 and 5 and make each an Area. Roll **6**.
    **Check (R5):** "Drop it and finish" appears only now, on the last keyword.
    Tap it.
    **Check:** the toast says "Dropped…". The walk ends; the facts chips read
    6 of 6 rolled with 3–6 Areas (R6). **Go to the room** opens on **Describe**.

### B3. Describe and encounter (R8, R9, R10, R31, R32, R35)
12. **Describe it** → write two sentences, mentioning something that is *not*
    one of your Areas.
    **Check (R8):** the text shows as a page with a drop cap. No new Area or tile
    was created. Tap the text or the quill to edit it.
13. The bar now says **Next: Encounter** → tap it.
    **Check:** a coach strip under the heading, the odds dial resting at 50/50,
    and the bar's Ask reading "(50/50)".
14. Drag the dial to **Likely**.
    **Check:** the bar's label changes to "(Likely)" and the band strip under the
    dial redraws. There is no second Ask button.
15. Tap **Ask: is there an encounter? (Likely)**.
    **Check (R35):** one die, the answer with "at Likely", the band strip with
    the roll marked. A double (11, 22 … 99) also shows a **Random Event** with
    two words (R32) — note it if it happens. Type a line under "What is it?" →
    **Good**.
16. Tap the Encounter stage again.
    **Check:** the answer card and your line are shown. **Clear** asks to
    confirm and names what is lost; cancel it.

### B4. Search — one roll per Area (R11–R19)
17. Go to **Search**.
    **Check:** the plan fills the width: named tiles, the General Area along the
    bottom wall, unsearched Areas as rows below with "Search this Area". The bar
    reads "Search: <first Area>".
18. Tap an unsearched tile.
    **Check (R11, R29):** the result sheet shows one d100, the Element, its glyph,
    the band strip with the roll marked and the Element's blurb. The tile turns
    solid in the find's colour (red for Unfortunate, green for Fortunate).
19. Tap the same tile again.
    **Check (R11):** it opens the card; it **never rolls again**. Look in Log to
    confirm there is exactly one Room Elements roll for it.
20. Search the remaining Areas from the tiles or the rows. As each Element comes
    up, check its own rule:
    - **Expected / Enhanced / Minimized** → the "face value, not the wish" note
      and its rule link (R15, guidance only).
    - **Fortunate / Unfortunate** → "use the obvious idea", plus
      **No idea — Discover Meaning**. Tap it, then **+ Action** or
      **+ Description**: each adds a word and never replaces one (R16, R33, R34).
    - **Random** → choose a table: Discover Meaning, Room Descriptors or Sock
      Drawer. A pair of words lands; doubles are kept (R17, R18).
    - **Multi-Element** → exactly two sub-Elements. A repeat or a nested
      Multi-Element is shown as replaced by Expected (R13, R14).
    The die decides which of these you see. Use the example rooms (step 3) to
    look at any you didn't roll.
21. On a searched card: **Roll a detail** → a Sock Drawer pair tied to that Area
    (R18). **Add a note** → saved on the card.
22. Before searching the General Area, check the header: the state chip reads
    Part searched and Search N/M counts explorable places (Areas + 1).
23. Tap the General Area strip on the plan (or **Search the General Area**).
    **Check (R19, R20):** one roll only. When it is the last search, the chip
    flips to **Fully explored** on this roll, not before. The General Area can
    take a detail too.
24. Areas heading → **Edit** → ✎ rename one, ▲▼ move one → Done.
    **Check (R26):** the tiles and the cards follow. The find itself can't be
    edited.

### B5. The + menu (R24, R36, R38)
25. Tap **+** in the bar → **Hidden things**.
    **Check (R24):** the "your own game's search mechanic first" text is always
    shown in full. Ask at any odds; the answer is kept in the list.
26. **+** → **Ask the GM** → type any yes/no question → Ask.
    **Check (R38):** kept on the room with odds, roll and answer.
27. **+** → **Notes** → write something.
28. While a sheet is open, tap the **Log** tab.
    **Check:** the sheet closes on leaving the screen. Go back.

### B6. Done, Record, journal (R22, R23, R28, R36)
29. Stepper → **Done**.
    **Check (R22):** the stamp, the named plan, summary tiles, no
    "conclusion" anywhere and no further roll. The goal question (Closer / No
    change / Further, house rule) is there. Tap one, then tap it again — it
    clears.
30. Stepper → **Record**.
    **Check:** everything on one page with a jump row; the bar walks the
    procedure.
31. **⋯** (room bar) → **Journal**.
    **Check:** the room as a chapter, every roll a line in the order made, with
    your sentences. An unwritten line shows a quiet "+ What is it?". Tap it,
    write, save.
32. **⋯** → **Read-aloud text** → Share or Copy. Paste it somewhere.
    **Check (R36):** it has the description, the encounter with its odds/roll,
    the finds, the hidden searches, the questions, your lines and the goal.
33. **⋯** → **Print this room** → Print (or save as PDF).
    **Check:** one page, the plan, no buttons, and a roll appendix that matches
    the Log.

### B7. Room 2 — three keywords, skipping (R7, R9, R21, R23, R28)
34. Done → **Next room in this crawl**.
    **Check (R28):** the budget picker defaults to whatever room 1 used. Pick
    **Three keywords** this time.
35. Walk three keywords (one Area each). Then, on Encounter, tap
    **Skip the question**.
    **Check (R9):** "Not asked for this room", with "Ask it after all".
36. Search **one** Area only, then stepper → Done.
    **Check (R21, R23):** the state is Part searched, and the hint says that is
    a real, finished state. **Earlier room / Later room** move between rooms 1
    and 2.

### B8. Room 3 — leaving mid-walk, several spaces (R25, R27)
37. Next room: turn **"Does this room cover several spaces?"** on and fill the
    note; type a room type that isn't on the list (e.g. "Bathysphere").
    **Check (R25, R27):** both accepted. The note shows on the sheet and the
    print record.
38. Roll two keywords, then press the bottom **Play** tab.
    **Check:** home leads with **Continue** for this room. Tapping it brings
    you back to the walk exactly where you left it.
39. Finish the walk. Then open the crawl.
    **Check:** the map has three tiles with type, Areas searched and state; the
    open room is lit. **Journal** shows three chapters. **Print** prints one
    room per page.

## Part C — Mythic off (10 min) (R10, R16, R24, R37)
40. Settings → Content → turn **Use One-Page Mythic** off. Open a room's
    Encounter stage.
    **Check (R10, R37):** no dial. "Roll the question on your own tables and
    record" with a **not automated** badge. Record an answer.
41. Search an Area that comes up Fortunate or Unfortunate (or open one).
    **Check (R16):** "No idea? Ask a Fate Question or Discover Meaning on your
    own tables." There is no Discover Meaning button.
42. **+** → Hidden things → record by hand. Learn → Rules.
    **Check (R37):** the Mythic rules are gone from the library. Turn Mythic
    back **on**; they return.

## Part D — Log and distribution (5 min) (R29, R39)
43. **Log**.
    **Check:** grouped by room, each room heading links to the room, keyword
    walks collapsed into one row of dice, Mythic and house-aid rolls badged. The
    filter row scrolls sideways and fades at its edge.
44. **Distribution**.
    **Check (R39):** ten bands with an expected line, and the 10×10 face grid.
    Its total counts every roll ever made, even past the 200-roll log cap.

## Part E — Settings and safety (10 min) (process rule 11)
45. Theme System / Light / Dark; text size Normal / Larger / Largest.
    **Check:** everything stays readable; the room bar and pinned bar never
    cover content.
46. Turn **Show house-aid suggestions** off.
    **Check:** no room-type list, no Roll a room type. Turn it back on.
47. **Your data** → Export JSON (and the text export). **Check my data**.
48. **Undo and erase** → **Undo: …** (the last thing you did).
    **Check:** it is undone and named in the toast.
49. **Erase everything** → confirm → tap **Undo** on the toast.
    **Check:** everything is back. Then Import the JSON from step 47 on top.
    **Check:** nothing duplicated or lost; the face counts survive.

## Part F — tablet and offline (optional, 10 min)
50. On a tablet or a wide window (≥760px): a room.
    **Check:** the stepper becomes a rail on the left; Search shows the plan
    beside the cards; the walk shows the deck beside the plan; Distribution
    shows the chart beside the face grid.
51. Install to the home screen, switch to airplane mode, open it.
    **Check:** it boots and plays offline; rolls still work.

## Things the die decides — note them if they happen
- A **double** on any Mythic question → Random Event (R32).
- **Multi-Element**, and a repeat inside it → Expected (R13, R14).
- A **Random** find → the Meaning table choice (R17).
- A **double** on a Sock Drawer pair → kept, not rerolled (R18).
